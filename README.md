# Alidade playground and Vigia (built files)

The built files of two pages, for trying them on a phone or, with their simulators, on a laptop:

- the playground of the Alidade skeleton (the sighting flow), at the root;
- Vigia, an app on that skeleton that crosses compass sightings of smoke into a position with its uncertainty, under `vigia/` (open `vigia/?mode=simulator` on a laptop).

Every scene in them is synthetic. Neither page calls or notifies anyone. The source is not in this repository.
